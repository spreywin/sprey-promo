Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sprey Processing, crypto payments, and getting started

Sprey Processing is designed around a non-custodial model. Payments are processed through your configured payment infrastructure, and Sprey does not hold your customers' funds.

Sprey Processing is built on BTCPay Server, an open-source cryptocurrency payment processor.

You need a Sprey Processing subscription and a compatible payment setup. The exact requirements depend on your chosen payment methods and integrations.

All features and tools included in your selected subscription are available without separate feature tiers. The subscription term determines your billing period and applicable discount.
